## Full description

**Become the person you want to be — one habit at a time.**

Streakly turns habit building into a game you actually want to win. Check in every day, grow your streak 🔥 and watch yourself change.

**🔥 THE STREAK THAT KEEPS YOU GOING**
Every completed day extends your streak. The longer it gets, the less you want to break it. That's the secret of disciplined people — now it's yours.

**🃏 THE JOKER THAT SAVES YOUR STREAK**
Missed a day? Your monthly joker rescues your streak. One accident shouldn't erase 30 days of effort.

**⏰ REMINDERS THAT HIT AT THE RIGHT TIME**
Pick a time for every habit. And if your streak is in danger at night, Streakly warns you before it's too late.

**🏆 MILESTONES WORTH CELEBRATING**
7 days, 30 days, 100 days… every milestone is a celebrated win. Share your streak card to Instagram or TikTok and inspire your friends.

**🎯 30-DAY CHALLENGES**
Pick a habit, hold the line for 30 days, and prove to yourself you can stick to anything.

**🧠 BECOME SOMEONE**
After 21 days in a row, a habit becomes an identity. You don't "do" workouts anymore — you ARE someone who works out.

**📊 STATS THAT SHOW YOUR PROGRESS**
Activity heatmap, calendar, success rates, records. Your discipline, visualized.
